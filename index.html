Can acoustic emissions reveal friction and wear?

Combined experimental tribology, signal analysis and machine learning to test whether microscopic elastic waves can support lubrication monitoring, fault detection and predictive maintenance. Rigorous validation exposed when models learned experimental conditions rather than transferable physics.
